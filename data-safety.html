Data Safety

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This page provides a clear summary of what data PillCare Reminder collects, why it is collected, and how it is handled. This information corresponds to our Google Play Data Safety declaration.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with advertisers. We do not use your data to train AI models without your consent. We do not collect financial information, biometric data, or the contents of your messages.

Data security

All data is encrypted with 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest. Access is controlled by your Google account authentication. Our team cannot read your health records.

User controls

You can access, correct, or delete your data at any time. Account deletion removes all data within 30 days. See our account deletion page for instructions.
