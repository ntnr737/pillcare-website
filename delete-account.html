Account deletion

Last updated: July 8, 2026

You can request deletion of your PillCare Reminder account and associated app data from inside the app or from this page.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app, open Profile or Settings.
  2. Select Account.
  3. Choose Delete account and follow the confirmation steps.

If you cannot access the app, email us from your registered email address at support@pillcare.in with the subject "Delete PillCare Reminder account".

What gets deleted

  • Your app account profile.
  • Medication schedules, reminder preferences, refill notes, and adherence history.
  • Caregiver sharing connections associated with your account.

What may be retained

Some limited records may be retained when needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. These are kept only for as long as necessary.

Processing time

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days after verifying the request. Backup copies may take additional time to expire from routine backup systems.
