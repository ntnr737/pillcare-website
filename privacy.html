Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PillCare Reminder handles information when you use our medication reminder app and related website.

Who we are

PillCare Reminder is provided by League of X Technologies Pvt. Ltd. The app helps users create medication schedules, receive reminders, and track routine adherence.

Privacy contact: privacy@pillcare.in

Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, and login information.
  • Medication reminder details including medicine name, dosage notes, reminder times, refill notes, and adherence history.
  • Caregiver or family-sharing details if you choose to invite or notify someone.
  • Device and app information such as device type, app version, crash logs, and diagnostics.
  • Support messages you send to us, including any contact information you include.

How we use information

  • To provide medication reminders, schedules, refill alerts, and dose tracking.
  • To sync your account and preferences across your devices.
  • To send notifications that you request or enable.
  • To provide customer support, fix bugs, and improve reliability.
  • To comply with legal obligations and platform policies.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data. We may share limited information only when needed to operate the app, such as with secure cloud hosting, authentication, analytics, and notification service providers. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Health information

PillCare Reminder is a reminder and organisation tool only. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and should not replace advice from a doctor or qualified healthcare professional.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest, stored on certified enterprise cloud infrastructure with global security standards.

Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the app, meet legal requirements, and resolve disputes. You can request account and data deletion at any time.

Request account deletion

Children

PillCare Reminder is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we collected data from a child without consent, we will take steps to delete it.

Compliance

PillCare complies with applicable data protection laws worldwide, including India's DPDP Act 2023, the EU's GDPR, and equivalent frameworks.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. The latest version will always be posted here with the updated date.
