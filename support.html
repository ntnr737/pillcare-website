Reminder not working?

Check that notification permission is granted in your phone settings. Go to Settings → Apps → PillCare Reminder → Notifications → Allow all. Also ensure battery optimisation is not restricting background activity.

Safety notice

PillCare Reminder is a reminder and organisation tool only. It does not provide medical advice. If you are unsure about a medication, missed dose, side effect, or health emergency, contact a qualified healthcare professional or call your local emergency number immediately.
